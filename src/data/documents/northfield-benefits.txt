Northfield Health — Employee Benefits Guide

Health benefits. You may choose between two medical plans for employee-only coverage:
- PPO 500: your cost $92.31 per paycheck ($2,400 per year); $500 deductible; 20% coinsurance; $3,000 out-of-pocket maximum. If you do not make an election, you will be enrolled in the PPO 500.
- HSA Plan 3000: your cost $23.08 per paycheck ($600 per year); $3,000 deductible; 20% coinsurance; $5,000 out-of-pocket maximum. Northfield deposits $1,000 per year into your Health Savings Account when you enroll in this plan.

Life insurance. Basic life insurance equal to 1x your annual salary is provided at no cost. You may purchase supplemental coverage up to 5x salary.
